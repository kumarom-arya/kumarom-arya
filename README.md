### 👨‍💻 About Me

I am a B.Tech Computer Science student specializing in Artificial Intelligence at Parul University (currently in my 6th semester with a 6.42 CGPA). I am deeply passionate about high-performance computing, IoT, and building intelligent, AI-driven applications. 

- 🔭 **Currently working on:** 
  - **Bio-Logix:** A risk-adaptive dynamic waste management system utilizing IoT sensors and Edge AI running locally on an ESP32 microcontroller.
  - **Lotus Fields:** Developing the e-commerce platform and brand identity for a startup exporting flavored Makhana and other GI-tagged traditional products from Bihar.
- 🌱 **Tech Stack:** Java, JavaScript, C/C++, Spring Boot, Node.js, Express.js, and the MEAN stack.
- ⚡ **Fun fact:** When I am not writing code or building hardware, I am probably traveling! I have explored 10 different states across India, with my most recent adventures being trekking in Nainital and ringing in the New Year in Mount Abu.
